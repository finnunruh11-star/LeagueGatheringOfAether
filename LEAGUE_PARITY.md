# CusOba vs. League of Legends

CusOba recreates the basic Summoner's Rift loop, not a single League patch. Its biggest differences are jungle objectives, vision tools, the item economy, respawn pacing, and how casts and attack-move are issued. The 2D presentation and support for custom characters and private bot lobbies are deliberate differences.

**Scope:** PC League's standard Summoner's Rift versus CusOba's Aether Rift and offline Practice. Other CusOba maps are called out where relevant. The CusOba side was checked against the working tree at `e6aa2c4` plus local changes present during the audit. The League side compares established systems and labels historical examples; it is not a certification against the latest live patch. Riot's reference pages were unavailable during this audit. Findings based on code are not claims of a visual or gameplay playtest.

## Mechanics and match pacing

| Area | CusOba | Difference from League and why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Map and lanes | Aether Rift is 14,800 × 14,800 with a 180°-mirrored layout, custom wall and brush polygons, three lanes and two river pits. See [Rift map data](maps/rift.json) and [map logic](scripts/world/map.gd). | The landmarks are familiar, but matching dimensions and layout do not make choke widths, ward spots, pathing, travel times or wall hops interchangeable. |
| Jungle objectives | Scuttler, four elemental drakes, Dragon Soul, Elder and Baron are implemented. The next drake is randomly picked from elements other than the previous one; Soul uses the fourth drake secured by the team. Baron starts at 20:00 and respawns after six minutes. No Herald, Voidgrubs, jungle plants, or elemental terrain changes were found in this map or its objective code. See [jungle rules](scripts/world/jungle.gd) and [Rift map data](maps/rift.json). | League has used additional early top-side objectives and jungle plants, and its elemental Rift system ties the terrain and subsequent drakes to one element. Exact objectives and schedules depend on the comparison season. Here there are fewer reasons to contest the upper pit early, and dragon kills never reshape routes. |
| Camp clears and Smite | All six standard camps start at 1:30, use camp leash distances, and scale with average champion level when spawned. Smite is a single 600 true-damage spell with a 60-second cooldown and no upgraded damage tier in its definition. See [jungle rules](scripts/world/jungle.gd), [monster AI](scripts/units/monster.gd), and [Smite](summoners/smite.json). | League's modern jungle progression upgrades Smite. CusOba's objective secure threshold stays at 600, and League clear routes and reset timings should not be assumed to transfer. |
| Turrets and base | Turrets prioritize nearer non-champions unless they pick up champion aggro. Shots deal fixed fractions of minion max health and ramp by 30% per consecutive hit against the same champion, up to five increments. Nexus access depends on an inhibitor being down and both Nexus turrets being dead. No outer turret plate payout or plate-specific defense rules were found. See [turrets](scripts/units/tower.gd) and [structure protection and payouts](scripts/world/world.gd#L690). | The siege sequence is recognizable, but League's plate economy and turret behavior affect when to hit towers, when to recall, and which dives work. Do not copy a League tower last-hit or dive calculation without testing it here. |
| Waves and XP | Aether Rift spawns 3 melee and 3 ranged minions from 1:05 every 30 seconds; cannons increase in frequency after 15 and 25 minutes, and downed inhibitors spawn supers. Shared lane XP divides a fixed pool of 120% of the solo award among nearby recipients. See [Rift wave configuration](maps/rift.json) and [wave and XP rules](scripts/world/world.gd#L565). | These resemble classic Rift timings, not a guarantee of current-patch timing or XP breakpoints. The shared XP rule is explicit and should be tested separately for solo, duo and grouped lanes. |
| Levels and respawn | Level cap 21, with skill points only through 18. Normal lane-map respawn is `4 + 1.25 × level` seconds (26.5s at level 18, 30.25s at 21); maps with no lanes use 3s. See [champion progression](scripts/units/champion.gd#L1448) and [respawn rule](scripts/world/world.gd#L686). | Standard Rift caps at 18 and uses longer late-game death windows. CusOba gives most champions extra stat levels and less time to convert a late kill into an objective or Nexus. |
| Gold and snowballing | Champion kills give a flat 300g and recent damaging assists split 150g. Non-inhibitor structure deaths give 50g to each opposing player, with a separate per-unit reward path for any structure with a `gold_value`. No first-blood, shutdown, kill-streak or objective-bounty payout was found in the reward path. See [reward constants](scripts/world/world.gd#L9) and [death payouts](scripts/world/world.gd#L748). | League's corresponding payouts are situational. CusOba gives less reason to focus a fed bounty target or trade for a bounty objective. |
| Shop and items | 185 item JSON files mix the patch 10.23–13.24 era; a one-Mythic limit is enforced. Builds can be edited per character and optionally purchased automatically when eligible. Practice lets you shop anywhere. See [item design](README.md#items), [purchase rules](scripts/units/champion.gd#L748), and [shop UI](scripts/ui/shop.gd). | League removed the Mythic tier in 14.1. A League build guide can recommend missing items or combinations this engine rejects. Autobuy can also change the normal rhythm of shopping and learning build components. |
| Vision and wards | Team vision checks walls and brush, and the host can disable map fog. Brush remains a hiding mechanic even when map fog is disabled. Control Wards exist as an item; no standard free warding trinket, sweeper or blue trinket slot was found. See [fog and brush](scripts/world/fog.gd), [Control Ward](items/control_ward.json), and [input bindings](scripts/controls.gd). | The lack of League's trinket kit matters more than whether the fog looks similar: it changes who can place or clear vision, when they can do it, and how much it costs. |
| Spells, runes and champions | Summoners are configurable JSON; for example, Teleport unlocks minions/wards at 10:00 and can be canceled by recasting. The repository describes its rune roster as 26.23 plus legacy choices; effects run on CusOba's rules, and several rune actions are accessed from Tab. The roster is mostly custom, with a Zeri recreation whose pellets, terrain behavior, item interactions and visuals are not exact. See [Teleport](summoners/teleport.json), [runes](scripts/runes.gd), [Zeri implementation](scripts/units/zeri_kit.gd), and [roster and rune notes](README.md). | The engine combines different eras rather than reproducing one patch. Matching spell, rune or champion names does not imply matching damage, cooldowns or interactions. |

## Controls and match feel

| Area | CusOba difference |
|---|---|
| Casting | Q/W/E/R aim on press and cast on release, or cast early with left-click; right-click cancels an armed cast. Self-targeted summoners or item actives can fire on press. No equivalent per-spell choice of League-style normal and immediate quick cast was found in settings. This changes combo timing even if both games show Q/W/E/R. See [input handling](scripts/world/world.gd#L925) and [settings](scripts/ui/settings_menu.gd). |
| Attack-move | Default `A` directly issues attack-move at the cursor. The `A` then left-click flow is optional; the selection preference can favor the cursor or the champion. Right-click is still move/attack and repeats while held. See [attack-move preferences](scripts/game.gd) and [match input](scripts/world/world.gd#L925). |
| WASD | CusOba's preset uses Q/E/R/F for abilities, Space/Shift for summoners, and X for attack-move. Importantly, the left-click handler first tries to **inspect** a directly clicked visible unit and only falls back to attack-move when it picks none. The settings describe left-click as attacking; the handler means a click directly on an enemy can inspect without issuing an attack. This is a source-backed finding, not a manually reproduced click test. See [bindings](scripts/controls.gd) and [left-click handler](scripts/world/world.gd#L953). |
| Camera and targeting | CusOba starts locked on fresh settings, supports Y **and** Z for lock toggle, and uses a 2D camera at 0.8× default zoom (wheel limits 0.45×–1.8×). Some abilities can pull the camera toward their target. Gameplay, pathing and selection radii are separate; an equally long nominal attack range does not imply the same clickable area or pathing around a target. See [camera settings and scale notes](README.md#league-scale-balance-branch), [camera behavior](scripts/world/world.gd#L1162), and [unit geometry](scripts/units/unit.gd). |
| Bindings and communication | The classic layout shares many familiar keys, but the README's “every key” claim is broader than implementation: left/right click, wheel, arrow-key panning, Escape and F1–F4 are fixed. No in-match League-style ping wheel or team chat was found in the inspected match UI/input. See [bindable actions](scripts/controls.gd) and [HUD](scripts/ui/hud.gd). |

## Visuals, audio, and modes

| Area | Difference |
|---|---|
| Battlefield | CusOba renders a 2D pixel-art battlefield with generated ground textures and cliff faces, sprite scenery and Node2D draw layers. League uses a 3D battlefield and character models viewed from an angled camera. Depth cues, silhouettes, and readability at different zoom levels will differ even where map geometry is similar. See [project renderer](project.godot) and [map art](scripts/world/map_art.gd). |
| Units, effects and sound | Minions use baked sprites, turrets/Nexus use SVG art plus engine-drawn elements, and champions can use their own sprite sheets. The engine has shared hit/zone effects and synthesized sound fallbacks, not League's authored 3D animations, skins, audio and effects. Compare gameplay telegraphs and timings rather than expecting assets to look or sound alike. See [minion art](assets/minions), [structures](scripts/units/tower.gd), [combat effects](scripts/combat/fx.gd), and [sound generation](scripts/sfx.gd). |
| HUD | The ability bar, inspected-unit stats, scoreboard, camp timers and minimap are CusOba's own UI. It has dedicated build/autobuy controls and rune actions on Tab rather than reproducing the League HUD/shop. The audited working tree also had an optional health-bar accessibility palette with gold self, blue allies, orange enemy plus a diamond, and gray neutrals. Its contrast and coverage were not measured. See [HUD](scripts/ui/hud.gd), [shop](scripts/ui/shop.gd), and [unit overlays](scripts/units/unit.gd). |
| Practice and alternate maps | CusOba uses an offline bot lobby with three bot difficulties, a separate Training Arena with DPS dummies, and F1–F4 for XP levels, gold, cooldown/mana reset and max ranks. Two Lanes and Bridge are custom maps, not League rulesets (in particular, Bridge is not ARAM). This is a different workflow from League's in-match Practice Tool command panel. See [Practice and maps](README.md) and [cheat input](scripts/world/world.gd#L991). |
| Multiplayer and modding | A player hosts the authoritative game; guests join by IP, and bots can replace disconnects. The host can distribute custom characters and approve guest suggestions. No League-style matchmaking, ranked progression or draft/ban flow was found. This is a private sandbox, not League's account and queue experience. See [network and lobby](scripts/game.gd), [lobby UI](scripts/ui/lobby.gd), and [character modding](characters/MODDING.md). |

## Verification and next comparisons

- **Tested:** Godot 4.7.1 `--headless --path . -- --auto=practice --map=arena --inputtest` passed with a clean settings profile. It covered camera toggles, rebinding/reset, WASD movement, Flash and attack-move. An initial run inherited saved WASD settings and failed two assertions that expected the classic layout; the clean-profile run did not reproduce those failures. The test did not cover direct left-click targeting in WASD.
- **Not visually verified:** camera framing at matching resolutions, brush and fog transitions, health bar and spell readability in teamfights, audiovisual timing, and guest input latency. The findings on those systems describe implementation, not observed side-by-side quality.
- **First checks for a follow-up:** (1) compare a direct enemy left-click with a nearby ground click in WASD and record the issued command; (2) compare press/release, cancellation, and attack recovery with the same cast setting; (3) time the first two waves, a camp clear, ward placement/detection, and a late death against one named League patch; (4) capture the same lane and teamfight framing at matched resolution to compare silhouette, HUD and spell readability.

League reference links for rechecking the baseline: [Riot's How to Play](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/how-to-play/), [patch 14.1 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-14-1-notes/), [League Wiki: Summoner's Rift](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Summoner%27s_Rift).
